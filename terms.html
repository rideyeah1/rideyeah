Cancellations, refunds and no-shows

You may cancel through the link in your confirmation, by chat, or by contacting us at info@rideyeah.com or +1 (805) 660-6439. The charge depends on how far before the scheduled pickup time we receive your cancellation:

Cancelled Charge
6 hours or more before pickupFull refund
Between 2 and 6 hours before pickup50% of the ride price charged
Between 1 and 2 hours before pickup75% of the ride price charged
Less than 1 hour before pickup, or no-show100% of the ride price charged

A no-show is when the passenger cannot be found at the pickup location and does not answer our calls or messages after the included waiting time has passed. If your plans change, tell us as early as you can — it is the only way to keep the charge down.

Refunds go back to the card used for the booking and appear in 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.

Waiting time

Every reservation includes complimentary waiting time:

  • 30 minutes for pickups at a street address, hotel or any non-airport location.
  • 60 minutes for pickups after a domestic flight.
  • 90 minutes for pickups after an international flight.

The clock starts at the scheduled pickup time. For airport pickups we track your flight, and the clock starts when the aircraft arrives at the gate, so a delayed flight does not cost you waiting time. Waiting beyond the included time is charged at the rate shown at booking (see the table above). If you know you will be late, message or call us and we will do our best to hold the vehicle.

Children and car seats

California law requires children under 8 years old to ride in a car seat or booster seat appropriate to their age and size, and children under 2 to ride rear-facing. Please tell us when booking if a child will be travelling. The parent or guardian provides and installs the car seat; the chauffeur cannot install it for you. We may decline to start a ride if a child does not have the required seat.
