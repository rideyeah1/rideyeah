Luxury Hotels in Beverly Hills

Iconic five-star hotels in the heart of Beverly Hills — legendary service, Rodeo Drive shopping, and a polished transfer from LAX.

  1. Home
  2. Travel Guides
  3. Luxury Hotels in Beverly Hills

Beverly Hills is shorthand for Southern California luxury, and its hotels are part of the legend. From a pink Mediterranean landmark that has hosted Hollywood royalty for over a century to sleek modern flagships steps from Rodeo Drive, this is where five-star service, fine dining, and impeccable design come together.

When choosing a luxury hotel in Beverly Hills, think about location within the city — the Golden Triangle puts you in walking distance of Rodeo Drive and top restaurants, while properties along Sunset Boulevard trade a little walkability for storied glamour and lush grounds. Also consider the experience you want: a classic, see-and-be-seen institution, or a quieter, contemporary retreat.

Beverly Hills sits about 12 miles northeast of LAX. The drive is usually 30 to 40 minutes but can stretch longer in heavy traffic, so a smooth, pre-arranged transfer is part of arriving in style. Every hotel below is a genuine luxury destination in its own right.

    Luxury

    Luxury

    9850 Wilshire Blvd, Beverly Hills, CA 90210

    12 mi from LAX 35 min Luxury

    A sleek, Art Deco-inspired flagship at the corner of Wilshire and Santa Monica boulevards, with a rooftop pool offering skyline views, a La Prairie spa, and a Jean-Georges restaurant. Modern luxury at its most refined.

  4. 4. The Maybourne Beverly Hills

  5. 5. L'Ermitage Beverly Hills

    Luxury

    9291 Burton Way, Beverly Hills, CA 90210

    12 mi from LAX 38 min Luxury

    An intimate, residential-feeling all-suite hotel prized for discretion and personalized service. A quieter alternative to the grand landmarks, with a rooftop pool and a loyal following among travelers who value privacy.

  6. 6. The Peninsula Beverly Hills

    Luxury

    12 mi from LAX 35 min Luxury
