Hotels Near Disneyland

Where to stay for the Disneyland Resort in Anaheim — from on-property magic to walkable Good Neighbor value, with an easy transfer from SNA.

  1. Home
  2. Travel Guides
  3. Hotels Near Disneyland

A trip to the Disneyland Resort in Anaheim runs smoother when your hotel is close to the parks. Staying nearby means shorter mornings, easy midday breaks back at the room, and the option to walk to the Disneyland and Disney California Adventure gates rather than navigating parking each day.

When choosing a hotel near Disneyland, decide first whether you want the on-property experience — themed rooms, early park entry perks, and a short walk to the esplanade — or the value and variety of nearby 'Good Neighbor' hotels along Harbor Boulevard and Katella Avenue. Also weigh proximity to the Anaheim Convention Center if your trip mixes a conference with park days.

John Wayne Airport (SNA) in Santa Ana is the closest major airport, about 14 miles south of the resort — typically a 25-minute drive. That makes SNA the most convenient gateway for a Disneyland trip, and a quick pre-arranged transfer gets your family from the terminal to the hotel without the hassle of car rental lines.

Our Picks (6)

  1. 1. Disney's Grand Californian Hotel & Spa

    Luxury

    1600 S Disneyland Dr, Anaheim, CA 92802

    14 mi from SNA 25 min Luxury

    The flagship on-property hotel, with Craftsman-style architecture and its own private entrance into Disney California Adventure. The most premium and convenient stay for guests who want to be steps from the parks.

  2. 2. Disneyland Hotel

    $$$$

    1150 W Magic Way, Anaheim, CA 92802

    14 mi from SNA 25 min $$$$

    The original resort hotel, full of classic Disney nostalgia, themed pools with waterslides, and a short walk through Downtown Disney to the park gates. A favorite for families wanting on-property perks.

  3. 3. Pixar Place Hotel

    $$$$

    1717 S Disneyland Dr, Anaheim, CA 92802

    14 mi from SNA 25 min $$$$

    A Pixar-themed on-property hotel (formerly Paradise Pier) celebrating characters from films like Toy Story and Up. Offers the on-site benefits of resort hotels with a contemporary, family-friendly design.

  4. 4. JW Marriott Anaheim Resort

    $$$$

    1775 S Clementine St, Anaheim, CA 92802

    14 mi from SNA 25 min $$$$

    An upscale full-service hotel a short walk from the resort district, with a rooftop bar, spa, and pool. A polished option for travelers wanting luxury amenities without on-property pricing.

  5. 5. The Westin Anaheim Resort

    $$$

    1030 W Katella Ave, Anaheim, CA 92802

    14 mi from SNA 25 min $$$

    A modern resort hotel across from Disney California Adventure and adjacent to the convention district, with multiple restaurants, a large pool deck, and rooftop dining. Strong for both park-goers and convention attendees.

  6. 6. Howard Johnson by Wyndham Anaheim (Good Neighbor Hotel)

    $$

    1380 S Harbor Blvd, Anaheim, CA 92802

    14 mi from SNA 25 min $$

    A family-favorite Good Neighbor hotel on Harbor Boulevard, walkable to the park entrance and known for its kid-friendly water-play area. A budget-conscious pick with easy access to the gates.
