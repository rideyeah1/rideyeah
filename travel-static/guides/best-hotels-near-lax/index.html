Best Hotels Near LAX Airport

Walkable, shuttle-served, and full-service hotels minutes from LAX terminals — ideal for early flights, late arrivals, and tight layovers.

  1. Home
  2. Travel Guides

If you have an early-morning departure, a red-eye arrival, or a long connection at Los Angeles International Airport, staying close to LAX can save you a stressful pre-dawn drive across the city. The hotels in this guide cluster along Century Boulevard and the surrounding Westchester area, putting you within a few minutes of the Central Terminal Area.

When choosing where to stay near LAX, weigh three things: how close you actually need to be, whether you want a shuttle or prefer a quick rideshare or pre-booked transfer, and the vibe you're after. Some travelers want a no-fuss airport hotel with a 24-hour shuttle; others want a polished, full-service property with a restaurant, pool, and bar to unwind before or after a flight.

All of the picks below are well-known, full-service hotels with a track record of handling airport travelers — meaning early check-outs, luggage storage, and staff used to flight schedules. Distances and transfer times are approximate and depend on traffic in the LAX horseshoe.

How to Get There from LAX

Most hotels along Century Boulevard run complimentary shuttles, but during peak arrival banks those shuttles can be crowded and run on a loop, so wait times vary. If you'd rather skip the shuttle line after a long flight, a pre-booked transfer with RideYeah meets you at the LAX-it rideshare lot and takes you straight to your hotel door — usually a five-to-ten-minute trip depending on traffic in the horseshoe.

Because these hotels are so close to the terminals, the transfer is short either way. The main consideration is timing: late at night or after an international arrival, having a driver already arranged means one less thing to coordinate when you're tired.

Choosing the Right Hotel

For the shortest possible transfer, prioritize the properties closest to the Central Terminal Area. For a more upscale, design-led stay, the newer builds tend to offer rooftop amenities and better dining. If you're traveling with a group or attending an event, the larger Marriott and Westin properties have the most space and meeting facilities.

Whichever you choose, confirm the hotel's shuttle hours if you have a very early or very late flight — and consider a pre-arranged transfer as a backup so you're never stranded waiting for the next loop.

Frequently Asked Questions

Most of the hotels in this guide are roughly 1 to 1.5 miles from the LAX terminals, along or just off Century Boulevard. That typically translates to a 5-to-10-minute transfer, though traffic in the terminal horseshoe can add a few minutes during peak times.
