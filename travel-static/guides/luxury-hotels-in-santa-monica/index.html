Luxury Hotels in Santa Monica

Beachfront five-star stays on the Santa Monica coast — ocean views, the pier at your doorstep, and a short, scenic transfer from LAX.

  1. Home
  2. Travel Guides
  3. Luxury Hotels in Santa Monica

Santa Monica is where Los Angeles meets the Pacific, and its best hotels make the most of it — ocean-view rooms, beachfront pools, and the pier, Palisades Park, and Third Street Promenade all within walking distance. It's a more relaxed, coastal counterpoint to Beverly Hills, with the same five-star polish.

When choosing a luxury hotel in Santa Monica, the first question is beachfront or downtown. Properties right on Ocean Avenue and the sand put the water at your doorstep, while hotels a few blocks inland near the Promenade trade the beach for shopping, dining, and a slightly easier rate. Either way you're minutes from the coast.

Santa Monica sits about 8 miles up the coast from LAX, usually a 20-to-30-minute drive. A pre-arranged transfer turns the arrival into part of the trip — a smooth ride to the beach instead of a scramble for rideshare. Every hotel below is a genuine luxury destination in its own right.

Our Picks (6)

  1. 1. Shutters on the Beach

    Luxury

    1 Pico Blvd, Santa Monica, CA 90405

    8 mi from LAX 25 min Luxury

    A beloved beachfront landmark built like a grand Cape Cod beach house, set directly on the sand. Coastal-elegant rooms, a celebrated spa, and a location right on the Santa Monica beach path make it a perennial favorite.

  2. 2. Hotel Casa del Mar

    Luxury

    1910 Ocean Way, Santa Monica, CA 90405

    8 mi from LAX 25 min Luxury

    A restored 1920s beach-club landmark on the sand, with opulent interiors, a grand lobby lounge, and oceanfront rooms. Sister property to Shutters and one of the most glamorous beachfront stays in LA.

  3. 3. The Fairmont Miramar Hotel & Bungalows

    Luxury

    101 Wilshire Blvd, Santa Monica, CA 90401

    8 mi from LAX 25 min Luxury

    A storied Ocean Avenue estate with lush gardens, private bungalows, and a giant landmark Moreton Bay fig tree. Steps from Palisades Park and the bluffs overlooking the beach, with classic full-service luxury.

  4. 4. Oceana Santa Monica, LXR Hotels & Resorts

    Luxury

    849 Ocean Ave, Santa Monica, CA 90403

    8 mi from LAX 28 min Luxury

    An intimate, all-suite boutique hotel on Ocean Avenue across from Palisades Park, with a sunny courtyard pool and a residential, design-forward feel. A quieter luxury option a short walk from the beach and the Promenade.

  5. 5. Santa Monica Proper Hotel

    Luxury

    700 Wilshire Blvd, Santa Monica, CA 90401

    8 mi from LAX 27 min Luxury

    A stylish downtown design hotel blending a historic 1920s building with a modern tower, known for its Kelly Wearstler interiors and a rooftop pool and restaurant with ocean views. Close to the Promenade and a short walk to the pier.

  6. 6. Viceroy Santa Monica

    Luxury

    1819 Ocean Ave, Santa Monica, CA 90401

    8 mi from LAX 25 min Luxury

    A polished Ocean Avenue hotel with two cabana-lined pools, a lively bar scene, and contemporary rooms a block from the beach. A see-and-be-seen choice near the pier and the southern end of the Promenade.

Getting from LAX to Santa Monica

Santa Monica is roughly 8 miles up the coast from LAX, generally a 20-to-30-minute drive depending on traffic on Lincoln Boulevard or the 405 and surface streets. It's one of the shorter luxury transfers from the airport, but coastal traffic can build in the afternoon, so leave a buffer for dinner or sunset plans.
