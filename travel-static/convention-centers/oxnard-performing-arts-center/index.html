Overview

The Oxnard Performing Arts Center (OPAC) is a flexible venue in central Oxnard that combines a main theater and auditorium with banquet and meeting spaces, making it suitable for both performances and gatherings. Centrally located in Ventura County, it serves as a hub for cultural and community events in the Oxnard area.

The center hosts a varied calendar that includes concerts and live performances, cultural showcases, conferences and corporate meetings, banquets, graduations, and community celebrations. Its mix of a sizable performance hall and event rooms lets it accommodate everything from stage productions to seated functions and small expos.

Visitors come for the events themselves and for Oxnard's broader appeal, including nearby beaches, Channel Islands Harbor, and a growing downtown dining scene. Set along the coast in Ventura County, the venue is well positioned for attendees combining an event with time in the area.

Getting There

The Oxnard Performing Arts Center is located at 800 Hobson Way in central Oxnard, a short distance from the 101 (Ventura) Freeway in coastal Ventura County. There is no airport in the immediate area, so most fly-in visitors arrive through a regional airport and drive in along the 101.

Santa Barbara Airport (SBA) is the nearest at roughly 35 miles up the coast, typically about a 45-minute drive. Hollywood Burbank (BUR) is around 50 miles to the southeast, often a good balance of distance and flight availability, and Los Angeles International (LAX) is the farthest common choice at about 60 miles, with travel times that depend heavily on freeway traffic.

Once in Oxnard, the venue is easy to reach from the 101, and Channel Islands Harbor, the beaches, and downtown Oxnard are all a short drive away. For larger events, follow on-site signage for parking and designated drop-off areas.

Nearby Hotels & Tips

Oxnard offers hotels both near the venue and along the coast around Channel Islands Harbor, giving visitors options close to the action or near the water. Staying nearby makes it easy to attend evening events without a long drive afterward.

The area's coastal setting means cooler, breezier evenings, so bring a layer for nighttime performances. Channel Islands Harbor, local beaches, and downtown Oxnard's dining are all close by for attendees who want to make a longer trip of it.
