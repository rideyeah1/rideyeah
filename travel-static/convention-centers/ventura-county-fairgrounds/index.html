Ventura County Fairgrounds

A beachfront fairgrounds and event venue in Ventura, home to the Ventura County Fair and year-round shows by the Pacific.

Overview

The Ventura County Fairgrounds is a sprawling beachfront event venue set right along the Pacific Ocean in Ventura, just off the city's historic downtown and pier. With exhibit buildings, open-air grounds, an arena, and a grandstand, it offers a flexible mix of indoor and outdoor space steps from the sand.

Best known for hosting the annual Ventura County Fair each summer, the fairgrounds also stages a year-round calendar of events, including swap meets, craft and trade shows, concerts, equestrian and livestock events, car shows, and community festivals. Its oceanfront setting and large footprint make it a versatile venue for everything from large public fairs to specialized expos.

Visitors are drawn by the rare combination of an event venue and a beach destination. With downtown Ventura's restaurants and shops nearby, the Ventura Pier and Promenade within walking distance, and the coastline right outside, attendees can easily pair an event with time by the ocean.

Getting There

The Ventura County Fairgrounds is located at 10 W Harbor Boulevard in Ventura, right off the 101 (Ventura) Freeway and immediately adjacent to the beach. There is no airport in the immediate vicinity, so most fly-in visitors arrive through one of several regional airports and drive in along the 101.

Santa Barbara Airport (SBA) is the nearest at roughly 30 miles up the coast, typically about a 40-minute drive. Hollywood Burbank (BUR) is around 45 miles to the southeast, often a good balance of distance and flight options, and Los Angeles International (LAX) is the farthest common choice at about 65 miles, with travel times that depend heavily on freeway traffic.

Once in Ventura, the fairgrounds are easy to reach off the 101, and downtown Ventura and the pier are within walking distance. During the county fair and other large events, expect heavier local traffic and follow signage to designated parking and drop-off areas.

Nearby Hotels & Tips

Ventura's downtown and beachfront have a range of hotels and inns, many within a short distance of the fairgrounds and the pier. Staying nearby lets you walk to the beach, the Ventura Promenade, and downtown dining without driving during busy event days.

The fairgrounds' oceanfront location means weather can be cooler and breezier than inland, so bring a layer. Downtown Ventura's restaurants, shops, and historic Main Street are a short walk away and make for an easy break between sessions.

Visitor Tips

  • During the Ventura County Fair, on-site and nearby parking fill early; arrive ahead of peak hours or use a drop-off.
  • The fairgrounds are right on the beach, so pack a layer for cooler, breezier coastal conditions.
  • Downtown Ventura and the pier are within walking distance, great for meals and breaks between events.
  • Weekend events and summer beach traffic can slow the 101 through Ventura, so build in extra travel time.
  • Confirm which buildings or outdoor areas your event uses, as the fairgrounds cover a large, multi-venue site.
