Overview

The Los Angeles Convention Center (LACC) is the premier exhibition and meeting venue in Downtown Los Angeles, offering roughly 720,000 square feet of exhibit space across its South and West Halls, along with dozens of meeting rooms and two main concourses. Its central location in the South Park district places it within walking distance of L.A. Live, Crypto.com Arena, the Peacock Theater, and a dense cluster of hotels, restaurants, and nightlife.

The LACC hosts some of the largest events in Southern California, from the Los Angeles Auto Show and major gaming and entertainment expos to anime, comic, and pop-culture conventions, consumer trade shows, corporate conferences, and esports tournaments. The venue's scale and Downtown setting make it a magnet for both industry professionals and large public crowds throughout the year.

Visitors come not just for the events but for everything around them. With Metro rail and bus connections nearby, a walkable entertainment district next door, and easy freeway access, the LACC is one of the most accessible large venues in the region, even though Downtown traffic and event-day congestion reward a little planning.

Getting There

The Los Angeles Convention Center sits at 1201 S Figueroa Street in the South Park area of Downtown LA, just off the 110 (Harbor) Freeway and a short distance from the 10 (Santa Monica) Freeway. Most visitors arriving by air come through LAX, roughly 18 miles southwest and typically a 35-minute drive off-peak, though event days and rush hour can push that well past an hour.

Hollywood Burbank Airport (BUR) is a convenient alternative to the north, around 16 miles away, and is often quicker and less crowded than LAX. Long Beach (LGB) and Ontario (ONT) are also within reach to the south and east, respectively, and can be good options depending on flight availability and where you're staying.

Metro rail and bus lines serve the area, with a station near L.A. Live, and rideshare drop-off and pickup zones are set up around the venue. During major shows, follow event signage and staff direction, since temporary traffic patterns are common.

Nearby Hotels & Tips

The South Park and L.A. Live district immediately around the LACC has a high concentration of hotels, ranging from large convention-oriented properties to boutique options, many within easy walking distance of the halls. Staying nearby lets you skip Downtown traffic entirely and walk to sessions.

Restaurants, bars, and entertainment at L.A. Live are right next door, so there's plenty to do before and after show hours without needing a car. If you're driving, compare on-site LACC parking with nearby structures, and arrive early on big event days.

Visitor Tips

  • On-site parking at the LACC fills quickly during major shows; arrive early or pre-book a transfer to avoid circling for a space.
  • Opening hours and the end-of-day rush are the busiest times for traffic and rideshare pickup, so build in extra time.
  • The South and West Halls are separated by Figueroa Street and connected by a concourse, so confirm which hall your event uses before arriving.
  • Downtown LA event days often overlap with games or concerts at the adjacent arena and theater, which compounds traffic, plan accordingly.
  • Metro rail is a practical option for getting into Downtown if you're staying along a transit line and want to skip parking.
