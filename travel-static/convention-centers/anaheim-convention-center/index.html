Overview

The Anaheim Convention Center (ACC) is the largest convention facility on the West Coast, with roughly 1.8 million total square feet, including more than 1 million square feet of exhibit space across its halls and the multi-level ACC North expansion. It sits directly across Katella Avenue from the Disneyland Resort, making it one of the most recognizable and conveniently located venues in Southern California.

The ACC hosts a packed calendar of major trade shows, conventions, and consumer events, from large industry expos and medical and tech conferences to fan conventions, sports and fitness shows, and gaming events. Its size lets it handle some of the biggest gatherings in the country, while the surrounding Anaheim Resort district provides hotels, dining, and entertainment within walking distance.

Visitors are drawn by the combination of a world-class venue and a built-in vacation destination. Many attendees pair a convention with a Disneyland visit, and the area's walkability, hotel density, and family-friendly atmosphere make Anaheim a popular choice for both business and leisure travel.

Getting There

The Anaheim Convention Center is located at 800 W Katella Avenue in the Anaheim Resort district, just off the 5 (Santa Ana) Freeway and near the 57 and 22 freeways. The closest airport is John Wayne Airport (SNA) in Orange County, about 14 miles south and typically a 25-minute drive, making it the most convenient gateway for most visitors.

Long Beach Airport (LGB) is another nearby option, roughly 18 miles away, and Ontario International (ONT) sits about 28 miles to the northeast. Los Angeles International (LAX) is the farthest of the common choices at around 35 miles, with drive times that vary significantly with traffic on the 5 and 405 freeways.

The Anaheim Regional Transportation Intermodal Center (ARTIC) connects train and bus service to the area, and a local transit network serves the resort district. On event days, follow signage for designated rideshare and shuttle zones around the venue.

Nearby Hotels & Tips

The Anaheim Resort district surrounding the ACC has one of the highest concentrations of hotels in California, with many properties within walking distance of both the convention center and Disneyland's main entrance. Staying nearby lets you walk to sessions and to the parks, avoiding traffic and parking entirely.

Dining, shopping, and entertainment are abundant in the area, and the Disneyland Resort is directly across the street. If you're combining business with a park visit, plan around peak crowds and consider purchasing park tickets in advance.

Visitor Tips

  • On-site convention center parking fills fast during major shows; arrive early or use a drop-off and pickup to skip the lines.
  • The resort district gets very busy when a big convention overlaps with peak Disneyland days, so allow extra travel time.
  • Many hotels are within walking distance of the ACC, walking can be faster than driving during event-day congestion.
  • Confirm whether your event is in the main arena, the exhibit halls, or the ACC North expansion, as the campus is large.
  • If you're pairing the convention with Disneyland, buy park tickets ahead and check park hours to plan your day.
