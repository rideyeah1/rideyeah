Pasadena Convention Center

A walkable, modern meeting and exhibition venue in the heart of historic Pasadena, near Old Town and the Civic District.

Overview

The Pasadena Convention Center is a modern, mid-sized venue in the heart of Pasadena, offering an exhibit hall, a grand ballroom, and numerous meeting rooms, all connected to the historic Pasadena Civic Auditorium. Its central location places it within easy walking distance of Old Town Pasadena, the Paseo shopping and dining district, and a range of hotels.

The venue hosts a diverse calendar of conventions, trade shows, corporate conferences, fan and pop-culture events, banquets, and performances. Its combination of flexible exhibition space and the landmark Civic Auditorium makes it a popular choice for events that mix exhibits with stage programming, and its walkable setting is a draw for attendees who want dining and entertainment close by.

Visitors appreciate Pasadena's relaxed, pedestrian-friendly atmosphere compared with larger Downtown LA venues. With tree-lined streets, plentiful nearby restaurants, and easy access to the broader Los Angeles area, the Pasadena Convention Center offers a more compact and convenient experience while still being well connected to regional airports.

Getting There

The Pasadena Convention Center is located at 300 E Green Street in central Pasadena, near the 210 (Foothill) and 134 freeways and a short distance from the 110. The closest airport is Hollywood Burbank Airport (BUR), about 13 miles west and typically a 25-minute drive, making it the most convenient gateway for most visitors.

Los Angeles International (LAX) is roughly 28 miles southwest, with drive times that vary widely with traffic, and Ontario International (ONT) sits about 30 miles east. Depending on flight options and where you're staying, any of these can work, but BUR is usually the quickest and least congested.

Pasadena is also served by the Metro A Line (light rail), with stations near the convention district, and rideshare drop-off and pickup are straightforward given the walkable street grid. During larger events, follow on-site signage for designated transport zones.

Nearby Hotels & Tips

Several hotels sit within walking distance of the Pasadena Convention Center, and Old Town Pasadena and the Paseo offer a dense, walkable mix of restaurants, cafes, and shops just a few blocks away. Staying nearby lets you skip driving and enjoy the city on foot.

Pasadena's compact center means most things are close together, so a single well-placed hotel can put you within walking distance of sessions, dining, and the Metro. If you do drive, compare on-site parking with the public structures around the Civic District.

Visitor Tips

  • On-site and nearby parking structures can fill during larger conventions; arrive early or use a drop-off to save time.
  • Old Town Pasadena is a short walk away and is great for meals and breaks between sessions.
  • The Metro A Line connects Pasadena to Downtown LA and beyond, a convenient car-free option for some attendees.
  • Weekend and festival events around Pasadena can add to local traffic, so check what else is happening on your dates.
  • The convention center connects to the historic Civic Auditorium, confirm which space your event uses before you arrive.
