Universal Studios Hollywood

Overview

Universal Studios Hollywood combines a working film and television studio with a full theme park, giving visitors a behind-the-scenes look at how movies are made alongside thrill rides and immersive lands. It sits in Universal City, tucked between Hollywood and the San Fernando Valley.

The park's signature experience is the Studio Tour, a narrated tram ride through real backlot sets and special-effects environments. Beyond the tour, guests can explore The Wizarding World of Harry Potter, Super Nintendo World, and a range of rides and shows themed to popular Universal films.

Adjacent to the park, Universal CityWalk offers free entry to restaurants, shops, live entertainment, and a movie theater — a popular spot to eat and unwind before or after your visit.

Getting There

Universal Studios Hollywood is just off the US-101 (Hollywood Freeway) in Universal City. The nearest airport is Hollywood Burbank Airport (BUR), only about 5 miles away — typically a 12-minute drive. Van Nuys (VNY) is also close, and LAX is roughly 40 minutes away depending on traffic.

The park has on-site parking and is also served by the Metro B Line (Red Line) Universal City/Studio City station, with a free shuttle connecting the station to the park entrance. CityWalk and the park share the same parking structures.

Visitor Tips

  • Buy tickets online in advance — dated tickets are often cheaper than at-the-gate prices.
  • Head to the most popular attractions, like Super Nintendo World, early in the day before lines build.
  • The park is built on a hillside; the Starway escalators connect the Upper and Lower Lots, so plan your route to minimize backtracking.
  • Consider a Universal Express upgrade on busy days to skip regular lines on most rides.
  • CityWalk is free to enter and a good place to grab a meal without a park ticket.
