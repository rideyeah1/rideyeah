Overview

Rodeo Drive is one of the most famous shopping streets in the world, running through the heart of Beverly Hills. Its name has become shorthand for luxury, lined with flagship boutiques from leading global fashion houses and high-end jewelers.

Beyond shopping, the street is a destination in itself. The cobblestone Two Rodeo lane evokes a European streetscape, and the surrounding Golden Triangle of Beverly Hills offers fine dining, landmark hotels, and elegant architecture. Even window-shopping and people-watching are part of the experience.

Rodeo Drive is also a popular spot for visitors hoping to glimpse a slice of the Beverly Hills lifestyle, with palm-lined streets, upscale cars, and a steady stream of photo opportunities. It is free to walk and explore at any time.

Getting There

Rodeo Drive sits in Beverly Hills, on the west side of Los Angeles between Santa Monica Boulevard and Wilshire Boulevard. The closest airport is Los Angeles International (LAX), about 12 miles away — roughly a 30-minute drive in normal traffic. Hollywood Burbank (BUR) and Van Nuys (VNY) are similar distances over the hill, often around 25 minutes.

Beverly Hills offers several public parking structures near Rodeo Drive, many with generous free initial periods, plus metered street parking. The area is compact and very walkable once you arrive.

Visitor Tips

  • Many Beverly Hills public parking structures offer a free initial period — a budget-friendly way to park.
  • The street is most photogenic in the morning light, before crowds build later in the day.
  • Two Rodeo, the cobblestone lane off the main street, is a favorite spot for photos.
  • You do not need to spend to enjoy Rodeo Drive — window-shopping and people-watching are free.
  • Pair your visit with a meal or coffee in the Golden Triangle to make an afternoon of it.
