Overview

SoFi Stadium is a cutting-edge indoor-outdoor stadium in Inglewood, anchoring the larger Hollywood Park entertainment district. Home to the NFL's Los Angeles Rams and Los Angeles Chargers, it is also one of the country's premier venues for major concerts and events.

The stadium is known for its sweeping translucent roof, the enormous dual-sided Infinity Screen video board, and an open-air design that takes advantage of Southern California's climate. It has hosted Super Bowls, College Football Playoff games, and world tours by top artists.

SoFi Stadium sits within the broader Hollywood Park development, which includes the YouTube Theater, restaurants, and a lake-and-park area. Its location near LAX makes it especially convenient for visiting fans flying in for a game or show.

Getting There

SoFi Stadium is in Inglewood, just a few miles from Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) — about 4 miles away and typically a 12-minute drive outside of event traffic. Long Beach (LGB) and Hollywood Burbank (BUR) are alternatives for travelers coming from the south or the Valley.

On event days, the area around the stadium sees heavy congestion, and parking must usually be purchased in advance. Many fans use rideshare drop-off and pickup zones, which the venue designates separately from parking lots to ease the post-event rush.

Visitor Tips

  • Buy parking in advance if you plan to drive — on-site parking sells out and prices rise for big events.
  • Arrive early to clear security and explore the Hollywood Park district before the event starts.
  • Use the designated rideshare zones for smoother pickup after the crowds let out.
  • Check the venue's clear-bag policy before you go to speed up entry.
  • The stadium is open-air in places; bring a light layer for cooler evening events.
