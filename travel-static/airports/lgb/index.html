Airport Overview

Long Beach Airport (LGB) is a smaller, easygoing airport known for its restored 1941 Streamline Moderne terminal and open-air boarding experience. It sits about 5 miles from Downtown Long Beach and offers a relaxed alternative to LAX for the South Bay and northern Orange County.

LGB operates under a noise ordinance that limits the number of daily flights, which keeps the airport calm and uncrowded. Travelers consistently rate it among the most pleasant airports in the region.

If your destination is Long Beach, the Queen Mary, or the beach communities nearby, LGB can save you the hassle of LAX while keeping you close to where you’re going.

Serves: Long Beach, Lakewood, Seal Beach, Los Alamitos, Northern Orange County, South Bay

Rideshare, Pickup & Drop-off

LGB’s compact layout makes pickups fast. Rideshare and pre-booked cars use marked curbside zones a short walk from the concourse.

Pickup zones

Drop-off
