Airport Overview

Palm Springs International Airport (PSP) is the main gateway to the Coachella Valley, serving Palm Springs and the surrounding resort cities of Palm Desert, Rancho Mirage, La Quinta, Indian Wells, and Indio. It sits just two miles east of downtown Palm Springs, so the drive from gate to hotel is short — one of the airport's signature conveniences.

PSP is best known for its open-air design: passengers walk outdoors between the terminal and the gates, with mountain views and desert sun on the way. A single passenger terminal feeds two concourses, and the airport stays compact and walkable even during its busy season.

Traffic at PSP is highly seasonal. Winter and spring — peak resort season plus the Coachella and Stagecoach festivals — bring a surge of flights, including seasonal Canadian and cross-country service. Summers are far quieter. Whenever you travel, the short distance into the valley makes a pre-arranged ride an easy, comfortable way to start a desert trip.

Serves: Palm Springs, Palm Desert, Rancho Mirage, La Quinta, Indian Wells, Indio, Coachella Valley

Rideshare, Pickup & Drop-off

PSP is compact, so getting picked up is simple. Rideshare and app-based pickups, taxis, and pre-booked cars all use the ground transportation area along the arrivals curb just outside the open-air terminal — no separate lot or shuttle to navigate.

Pickup zones

  • Ground transportation curb outside the terminal — rideshare, taxi, and pre-arranged pickups
  • Designated rideshare staging is signed near the terminal exit

Drop-off

Departing passengers are dropped at the terminal curb. Because the terminal is open-air and close to the gates, walks from the curb are short.

  • •During Coachella, Stagecoach, and peak resort weekends, curb traffic and rideshare waits spike — a pre-booked pickup avoids the surge and the line.
  • •It's a roughly 5-to-10-minute drive to most central Palm Springs hotels, so transfers here are quick once you're in the car.

Tips for Travelers

  • Pack for the walk: the terminal is open-air, so you'll be outside briefly between the gates and the building — pleasant in winter, hot in summer.
  • Book transfers early for festival weekends (Coachella and Stagecoach in April); demand across the valley peaks and cars get scarce.
  • Most Coachella Valley resorts are 10–30 minutes from PSP; downtown Palm Springs is the closest at about 5–10 minutes.
  • Summer is the valley's low season with reduced flight schedules and extreme heat — confirm your airline's seasonal service before booking.
  • The festival grounds (Empire Polo Club) are in Indio, roughly 25–30 minutes east of PSP.
