Airport Overview

Hollywood Burbank Airport (BUR), formerly Bob Hope Airport, is a smaller, far less hectic alternative to LAX for travelers heading to the San Fernando Valley, Hollywood, Burbank’s media studios, and Universal Studios. It’s roughly 12 miles from Downtown LA and just a short drive from Universal City.

Frequent flyers love BUR for its simplicity: short walks, quick security, and an easygoing layout. A new, modern replacement terminal is under development, but the airport’s signature convenience remains its biggest draw.

Because it’s compact and close to the studios and the Valley, BUR is often the smartest choice if your trip centers on Hollywood, Burbank, or Universal rather than the Westside.

Serves: Burbank, Hollywood, Universal City, Glendale, Pasadena, San Fernando Valley

Rideshare, Pickup & Drop-off

BUR’s small footprint makes pickups quick and painless compared with LAX. Rideshare and pre-booked cars use clearly marked curbside zones just outside the terminals — no long shuttle rides required.

Pickup zones

  • Designated rideshare pickup area at the curb outside the terminal
  • Pre-booked car services meet passengers curbside on the arrivals level

Drop-off

Departure drop-off is right in front of the terminal — one of the quickest curb-to-gate experiences in the LA area.

  • •Short walks and minimal traffic make BUR ideal for tight schedules.
  • •A pre-booked RideYeah pickup means your driver is waiting curbside when you land.

Tips for Travelers

  • BUR is the closest major airport to Universal Studios Hollywood — often a smarter choice than LAX for that trip.
  • Security lines are typically short, but the airport is compact, so gates fill quickly at busy times.
  • Choose BUR over LAX when your destination is the Valley, Hollywood, Burbank, or Pasadena to save significant drive time.
  • Most flights are domestic; international travelers usually connect through a larger hub.
