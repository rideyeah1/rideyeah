Airport Overview

Ontario International Airport (ONT) is the primary airport for California’s Inland Empire, about 38 miles east of Downtown Los Angeles. For travelers based in Riverside, San Bernardino, or the eastern LA suburbs, ONT is far more convenient than fighting traffic to LAX.

ONT has two passenger terminals (Terminal 2 and Terminal 4) connected landside, with a growing roster of domestic and international destinations. It’s one of the fastest-growing airports in the country, with shorter lines and easier parking than the coastal airports.

If your trip centers on the Inland Empire or the eastern side of greater LA, flying into ONT can save an hour or more of driving compared with LAX.

Serves: Ontario, Riverside, San Bernardino, Rancho Cucamonga, Inland Empire, Eastern LA County

Rideshare, Pickup & Drop-off

ONT offers straightforward rideshare and pre-booked car pickups at designated zones near the terminals, with far less congestion than LAX.

Pickup zones

  • Designated rideshare pickup areas outside Terminals 2 and 4
  • Pre-booked car services meet passengers on the arrivals level

Drop-off

Departure drop-off is curbside at each terminal’s upper level — quick and uncongested.

  • •ONT’s easy access makes it a strong choice for Inland Empire trips and onward rides to the desert or mountains.
