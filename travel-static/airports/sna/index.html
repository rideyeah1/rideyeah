Airport Overview

John Wayne Airport (SNA) is the primary airport for Orange County, located in Santa Ana about 14 miles from Disneyland and the Anaheim resort area. It’s the most convenient airport for visitors to Anaheim, Irvine, Newport Beach, and Costa Mesa.

SNA is modern and easy to navigate, with two main terminals (Terminals A, B, and C share one building) and a reputation for efficiency. A nighttime noise curfew limits late departures and arrivals, which keeps operations orderly.

If your trip centers on Disneyland, an Anaheim convention, or the Orange County coast, SNA almost always beats LAX on both distance and convenience.

Serves: Santa Ana, Anaheim, Irvine, Newport Beach, Costa Mesa, Orange County

Rideshare, Pickup & Drop-off

SNA handles rideshare and pre-booked car pickups at a designated area on the arrivals level, a short and well-signed walk from baggage claim.

Pickup zones

  • Designated rideshare pickup zone on the arrivals (lower) level
  • Pre-booked car services meet passengers curbside near baggage claim

Drop-off

Departure drop-off is on the upper (departures) level directly in front of the terminal.

Tips for Travelers

  • SNA is the closest major airport to Disneyland and the Anaheim Convention Center.
  • A nighttime noise curfew limits late flights — check departure times when booking.
  • Terminals A, B, and C are in one connected building, so transfers are simple.
  • For Orange County beaches like Newport and Laguna, SNA is far more convenient than LAX.
