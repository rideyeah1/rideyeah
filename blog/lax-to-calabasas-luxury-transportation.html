LAX to Calabasas Luxury Transportation

Airport Transfers · June 8, 2026 · 6 min read

Calabasas is one of the easiest Los Angeles communities to reach from LAX — about 30 miles and 40 minutes up the 405 and 101 — and one of the most particular about how its residents and guests arrive. Privacy, punctuality and a low-key presence matter here. This guide covers the smartest way to handle LAX to Calabasas transportation, what it costs, and what to expect from a private chauffeured SUV.

Why Calabasas calls for a private chauffeur

Between The Commons, the hillside estates and the gated communities off Mulholland and Las Virgenes, Calabasas is built around discretion. A shared shuttle or a random rideshare doesn't fit that brief. A private chauffeur does:

  • Discretion — one vetted, professional driver in an unmarked luxury black SUV, not a rotating cast of rideshare cars.
  • Gated-community access — your chauffeur has the address, gate and guard-house details in advance for a smooth arrival.
  • Predictability — a fixed price and a tracked flight, so nothing about the trip is improvised.
  • Space — room for up to 7 passengers and a weekend's worth of luggage.

The route and drive time

Most LAX → Calabasas trips run the 405 North to the 101 North, exiting around Las Virgenes or Lost Hills — about 40 minutes in normal traffic for the ~30-mile drive. It's a short, scenic run once you clear the airport, and your chauffeur watches live conditions to avoid the 101's known choke points through the Sepulveda Pass and Woodland Hills.
