Delete your account and data

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This page explains how to ask RideYeah (R Y Quiroz Luxury LLC) to delete your account and the personal data we hold about you. It applies to chauffeurs who use the RideYeah Chofer app and to passengers who booked with us.

How to request deletion

Send an email to info@rideyeah.com with the subject “Delete my RideYeah account” from the email address or phone number on your account, or call or text (805) 660-6439. Tell us whether you want the whole account deleted or only specific data (for example your profile photo or documents).

We confirm your identity by replying to the contact details on file, and we complete the deletion within 30 days. You will receive a confirmation when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Chauffeurs: your app account and sign-in, profile photo, uploaded documents (license, W-9, insurance, proof of address), device notification tokens and stored location history from trips.
  • Passengers: your contact details, saved addresses, notes and any card reference kept with our payment processor (the card itself is stored only by Stripe and is detached from your profile).

What we keep, and for how long

We are required to keep trip, payment and payout records for accounting, insurance and tax purposes. Those records are kept for up to 7 years in a form that is no longer linked to an active account, and then deleted. Nothing is used for marketing after your request.

Questions

Write to info@rideyeah.com. Our full Privacy Policy describes everything we collect and why.
