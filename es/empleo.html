Before you start

Text messages about your rides.

When you join RideYeah as a chauffeur, we ask for your mobile number and, if you agree, we send you operational text messages about the rides assigned to you — reminders to update a ride's status in the partner app, and questions about whether your passenger is in the car. You are asked to confirm yes or no during onboarding, and you can change your mind at any time.

  • Only about your own assigned rides — never marketing
  • Message frequency varies — it depends on how many rides you have
  • Message and data rates may apply, depending on your plan
  • Reply HELP for help, or STOP to stop the messages
  • We never share or sell your phone number

Saying no does not affect your work with us — we will call you instead. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
